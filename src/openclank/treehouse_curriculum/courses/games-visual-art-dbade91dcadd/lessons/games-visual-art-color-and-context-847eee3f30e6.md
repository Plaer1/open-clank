# The same color can look different

- Hue, lightness and chroma describe different aspects of appearance. A palette needs a hierarchy of use as well as a set of swatches.
- Surround the same middle-gray patch with dark and light fields. Compare appearance while keeping the patch's numeric value unchanged; neighboring colors can affect perception.
- Separate physical light, human perception, measured color representation and artistic use. Additive screen mixing and subtractive pigment mixing are different processes.
- Warm/cool labels are compositional language; they are not interchangeable with physical color-temperature measurements.

**Self-check:** Create or describe the two identical-patch panels, record what appears different, and propose a non-color cue for a critical game state.
