# Choose how to show depth

- Projection is geometry; depth cues are perceptual evidence; a pictorial style chooses how to use them.
- Perspective uses convergence and depth-dependent size. Orthographic and axonometric views can preserve useful measurements; flattened or multiple-viewpoint art can serve another goal.
- Occlusion, relative size, texture density, atmospheric contrast and motion parallax can imply depth. A cue depends on assumptions: a smaller object might simply be smaller.
- Separate attached shadow from cast shadow, broad diffuse shading from a sharp highlight, and surface texture from shape. Stylization can simplify these consistently.
