# Read the current source behind a snippet

- Search snippets help find evidence; the source's date and scope determine whether it answers the question.

**Fictional Practice Store evidence:** P1 policy v2, effective 2026-10-01, current: `Tools purchased from Practice Store may be returned within 14 days with receipt. Software is excluded.` P0 policy v1, effective 2025-01-01, expired 2026-09-30: `Tools may be returned within 30 days with receipt.` The search snippet says 30 days.

A tool was purchased October 10; the question is asked October 20. Ten days have elapsed, within P1's 14-day window if the receipt condition is met. These cards say nothing about fees. They are fictional practice facts, not a real retailer's policy.
