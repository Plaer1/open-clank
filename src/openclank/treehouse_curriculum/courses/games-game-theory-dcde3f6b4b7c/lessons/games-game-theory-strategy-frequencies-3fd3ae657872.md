# Payoffs depend on who is common

Optional practice.

- Symmetric table: A/A pays 3; A/B and B/A pay 0; B/B pays 2. A frequency is p.
- Fitness fA=3p, fB=2(1−p). Declared resampling rule p_next=p·fA/[p·fA+(1−p)·fB], denominator positive.

**Self-check:** Update p=1/2 twice, p=1/10 once and p=2/5 once. Explain why rare A can shrink despite its larger same-type payoff.
