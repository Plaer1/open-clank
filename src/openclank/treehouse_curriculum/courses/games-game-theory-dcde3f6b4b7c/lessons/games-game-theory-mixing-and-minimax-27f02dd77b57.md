# Randomize for a reason

- In matching pennies, the row player earns +1 when choices match and −1 otherwise; the other player receives the opposite payoff.
- Always choosing Heads can be exploited. A half-Heads, half-Tails mixture makes either pure response yield expected payoff 0.
- A pure strategy is a complete plan; a mixed strategy randomizes over plans. A behavioral strategy randomizes at decision points.
- For a zero-sum table, compare your worst payoff for each action before choosing a guaranteed payoff. Mixing can improve that guarantee; do not assume it must.

**Self-check:** Compute the row player's expected payoff from always Heads against an opponent choosing Heads with probability 0.75. Then compute it against 0.5.
