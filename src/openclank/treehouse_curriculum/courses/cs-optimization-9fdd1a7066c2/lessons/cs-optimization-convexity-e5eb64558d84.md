# Local and global optima

- A convex set contains the segment between any two of its points. The interval [0,2] is convex; {0,2} is not.
- A convex function lies no higher than the chord between points. Minimizing a convex function over a convex feasible set makes a local minimum global.
- Nonlinear does not automatically mean nonconvex; x² is nonlinear and convex.

**Try:** Compare minimizing x² over [−2,2] and restricting x to {−2,2}. Identify feasible sets and best values.
