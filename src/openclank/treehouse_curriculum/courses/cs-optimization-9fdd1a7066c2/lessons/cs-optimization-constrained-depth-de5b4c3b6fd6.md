# Bounds, multipliers, and sensitivity

- A feasible candidate supplies an upper bound for minimization; a valid lower bound limits how much better a solution can be. Matching bounds establish the optimum.
- For minimize x² with x≥1, find a lower bound that holds for every feasible x, then look for a candidate that reaches it.
- Lagrangian duality constructs bounds from constraints. Strong duality requires conditions; it is not automatic.
