# Know what your evidence establishes

- A screenshot establishes visible state at capture time. A tool receipt establishes the reported operation and scope. A test establishes behavior for its tested cases.
- Generated prose, a launched job or a green client-side animation alone cannot establish the intended external result.
- Report unavailable data and failed checks explicitly; do not relabel them as success.

An agent says “saved,” but reopening shows old content. The claim and observed result conflict.
