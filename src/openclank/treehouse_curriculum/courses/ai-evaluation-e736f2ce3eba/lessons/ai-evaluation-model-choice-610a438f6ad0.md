# Choose a feasible complete plan

A cheap or fast plan is useful only if it meets the task's acceptance conditions.

These fictional complete-plan observations are supplied; they are not independent model benchmarks.

| Plan | Complete cost | Elapsed time | Final quality |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A: correction, reads, worker, synthesis, storage | 5.35 credits | 12 seconds | 5/5 correct; zero unsupported |
| B: source set already in context | 11 credits | 6 seconds | 5/5 correct; zero unsupported |

Required quality: all five correct and zero unsupported. Budget and deadline ceilings are inclusive.

Choose for each case: budget 8/deadline 15; budget 14/deadline 8; budget 8/deadline 8. Changed case: A's final quality is only 4/5. Further case: budget 14/deadline 15 makes both financially and temporally feasible if the original quality observations hold.

The raw notice cases and outputs are absent, so use the quality values as supplied observations only. Different context and harness plans prevent a causal claim that one model is intrinsically better.
