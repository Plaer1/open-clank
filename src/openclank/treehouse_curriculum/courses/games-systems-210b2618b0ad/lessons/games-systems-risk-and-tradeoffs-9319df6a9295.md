# Choose the metric before the winner

- Loadouts can trade damage, reliability and resource use. A dominated option is no better on any chosen measure and worse on at least one.
- A: damage 8, cost 2; B: damage 6, cost 3; C: damage 10, cost 5. Maximize damage and minimize cost: A dominates B, but A and C trade off.
- Expected damage can hide risk: a guaranteed 5 and a 50% chance of 10 both average 5, yet behave differently near a one-hit survival threshold.
- Sensitivity asks whether a conclusion changes when a parameter changes. Robustness asks how it holds across specified uncertainty.

**Self-check:** Remove dominated options. Choose A or C under a cost cap of 3, then 6; state the objective and one uncertainty that could reverse the choice.
