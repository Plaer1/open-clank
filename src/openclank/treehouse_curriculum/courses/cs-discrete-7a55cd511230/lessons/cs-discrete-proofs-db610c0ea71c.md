# Proof and counterexample

- A universal claim needs a general argument; one counterexample can refute it.
- Direct proof: if n=2k, then n²=4k²=2(2k²), so an even integer has an even square.
- Contrapositive proves P→Q by proving not Q→not P. Contradiction assumes a claim's negation and derives impossibility.

**Try:** Prove the sum of two even integers is even. Refute 'all primes are odd'.
