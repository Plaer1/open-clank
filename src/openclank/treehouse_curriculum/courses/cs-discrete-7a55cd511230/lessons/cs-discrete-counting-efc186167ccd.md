# Count without duplication

- Multiply independent choices: 3 shirt choices × 2 hat choices = 6 outfits.
- Order matters for a sequence, but not for a committee. Choosing two of four people gives 4×3/2=6 pairs.
- Add disjoint cases; subtract overlaps when cases intersect.

**Try:** Count length-two codes from A,B,C with repetition allowed, then unordered pairs of distinct letters.
