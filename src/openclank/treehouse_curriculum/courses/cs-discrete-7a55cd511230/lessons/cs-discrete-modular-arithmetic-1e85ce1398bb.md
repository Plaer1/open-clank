# Remainders and divisibility

- a≡b mod m means m divides a−b. With modulus 5, 7 and 2 belong to the same remainder class.
- Euclid's algorithm repeats gcd(a,b)=gcd(b,a mod b) until b=0.
- Division modulo m needs an inverse; not every nonzero remainder has one.
