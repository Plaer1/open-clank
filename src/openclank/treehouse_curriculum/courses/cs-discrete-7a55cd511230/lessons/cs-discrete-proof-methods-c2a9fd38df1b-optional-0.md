P: n² even; Q: n even. Converse: n even→n² even. Contrapositive: n odd→n² odd. Let n=2k+1; n²=4k²+4k+1=2(2k²+2k)+1, odd. Parity's two exhaustive cases make not-even equivalent to odd. This direction gives an explicit representation to expand. Contradiction starts with n² even AND n odd. The converse happens to be true but is a different implication; finitely many examples do not prove a claim over all integers.
