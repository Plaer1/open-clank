∀x∃y R(x,y) may choose a different y for each x; ∃y∀x R(x,y) demands one shared y. On integers, ∀x∃y(y>x) is true but ∃y∀x(y>x) is false. A bound variable lies inside its quantifier's scope; a free variable needs a supplied value. De Morgan's law gives not(P and Q) = (not P) or (not Q); check all four truth-table rows. Equivalence means both implications; a necessary condition need not be sufficient.
