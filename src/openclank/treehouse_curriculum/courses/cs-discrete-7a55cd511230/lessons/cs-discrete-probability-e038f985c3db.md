# Probability, conditioning, expectation

- Assign nonnegative outcome probabilities summing to 1. Independence is an assumption to check, not a synonym for different events.
- Conditioning restricts to the observed event: P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B), when P(B)>0.
- Expected value is the probability-weighted average; it need not be a possible individual outcome.

**Try:** For a fair die conditioned on an even roll, find P(roll>3). For a payoff 0 with probability 3/4 and 8 with probability 1/4, find expectation.
