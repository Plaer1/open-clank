A generating function stores a sequence as coefficients: A(z)=a₀+a₁z+a₂z²+…. Multiplication combines choices by summing products whose exponents add to the target total. For two choices of 0 or1, (1+z)²=1+2z+z²: there are two ways to total 1. Try three such choices; check coefficient of z² is3. These are formal coefficients; evaluating an infinite series requires separate convergence care.
