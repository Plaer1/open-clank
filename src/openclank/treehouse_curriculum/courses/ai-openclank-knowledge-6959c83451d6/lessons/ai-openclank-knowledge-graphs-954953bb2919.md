# Read a graph without mistaking layout for data

- Graph visualizations expose relationships. Layout, fit and reset alter the view, not the underlying records.
- Graph owns the relevant Graph/Galaxy/Mind modes; historical names do not establish separate current launchers.

**Offline graph:** nodes A (open) and B (done). Edge A → B comes from a reference in A. Filter status=open hides B. Clearing the filter reveals B and the edge. Fit camera brings visible nodes into view without creating or deleting records.

A drawn edge needs a source relationship. Nearness on screen alone is not a documented connection.
