# Use the controls available to your role

- Help and personal settings do not grant system administration. Visibility can vary by account and deployment.

**Offline card:** account A can open Help and personal preferences. Users and System panels are unavailable. Another account's documentation shows those panels, but no grant is supplied for A.

Use A's available controls and record unavailable prerequisites. Changing a personal preference does not establish a server-wide change or new administrative role.

**Provider variant:** A's Open Clank session is valid, but A's separate Cedar provider credential is expired. A or its authorized provider-account owner diagnoses that provider access; personal app login does not repair it.
