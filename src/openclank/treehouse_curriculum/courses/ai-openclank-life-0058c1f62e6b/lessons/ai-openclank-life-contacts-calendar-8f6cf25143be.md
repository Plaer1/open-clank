# Confirm the person and the instant

- Resolve both identity and timezone before preparing an invitation. A calendar draft does not prove an invitation was delivered.

**Offline request, made Wednesday 2026-10-14:** `Meet Sam next Friday at 3.` Contacts Sam A and Sam B are distinct. Supplied confirmation selects Sam A, Friday 2026-10-16 at 15:00 with offset −10:00. Sam A's displayed offset for this event is +01:00. Draft E1 is unsent.

The confirmed instant is 2026-10-17 01:00 UTC, shown to Sam A as 02:00 at +01:00. Use these supplied offsets; this is not a general daylight-saving rule.
