# Optional worksheet: follow the asymmetric box

Use the asymmetric box from the lesson as a small import test. Record the source identity and revision or checksum, selected tool and settings, output identity and checksum, and the destination you chose. Compare the actual imported orientation, scale, materials, and animation behavior with your expected result. Identify what you observed and what you have not checked.

Suppose the first import is sideways or fails. Keep the failed result and its evidence distinct from the source. Decide whether to correct settings or the source, then explicitly retry with a new recorded output; do not silently overwrite the only original or claim the failed attempt succeeded. Use the existing Files and History/Lore records where available to recover or inspect a prior version.

Connect the chain in your notes: source → selected tools and settings → observed output → recovery evidence. If you choose to retain a lesson in Memery, review what is saved and its provenance; Graph evidence can help inspect relationships but is not a substitute for the source or History. These are separate existing authorities, not a second evidence ledger or a promise that every tool or recovery operation is available.
