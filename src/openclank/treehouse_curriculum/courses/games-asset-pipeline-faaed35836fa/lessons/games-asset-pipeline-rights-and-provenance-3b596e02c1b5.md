# Know what entered the asset

- Record creator, source, version, license/permission, modifications and required credit for every external component. Code, fonts, images and sounds can have different terms.
- A generated image is an input to review, not proof of permission, consistent perspective, usable transparency or a complete game-ready asset.
- Preserve original source and exported derivatives with a short reproducible transformation record. Keep private prompts/data out of a public package unless deliberately included.
- An evaluation record can compare an assisted edit against the original: changed pixels, intended change, unintended change and final human decision.
