Linear logic treats selected assumptions as resources rather than freely duplicable facts. Constructive logic asks for constructions/proofs and does not generally adopt classical excluded middle. Many-valued/fuzzy systems, relevance/paraconsistent logics, deontic obligations, defaults and belief revision are distinct extension families; their technical rules are outside this exercise. Default 'birds normally fly' can be withdrawn after learning an exception; probabilities express uncertainty rather than automatically defining graded truth.
