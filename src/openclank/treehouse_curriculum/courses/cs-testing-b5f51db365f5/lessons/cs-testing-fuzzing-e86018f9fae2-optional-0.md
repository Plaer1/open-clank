Empty input correctly yields []; `"1,"` should raise ValueError and the broken parser already rejects it. `"+1"` is rejected by the contract but accepted by the implementation. Shrinking `" +1 "`→`"+1"` preserves that failure; `"+1"`→`"1"` removes it because1 is valid. Overlength whitespace, too many fields and out-of-range numbers provide other contract checks. Do not treat all rejected inputs as bugs.
