# Test the uncertain idea

- Choose the smallest prototype that can answer a question: paper rules for choices, a graybox for navigation, a technical spike for a risky effect.
- State a prediction: “the exit marker will be noticed before the side room.” Observe a play attempt, then separate what happened from your explanation.
- Change one major variable before the next attempt. A vertical slice tests combined production quality; it is expensive evidence for a simple rules question.

**Self-check:** Run one paper or digital attempt yourself, or with a willing tester. Submit the hypothesis, observed action sequence, one confound and one revision.
