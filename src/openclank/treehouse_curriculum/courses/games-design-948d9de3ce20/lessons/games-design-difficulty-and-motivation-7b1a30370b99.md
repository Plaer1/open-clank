# Separate kinds of difficulty

- Complex rules, precise inputs, uncertain information and harsh punishment create different demands.
- An assist can enlarge an input window without changing the puzzle; a hint can reduce uncertainty without slowing movement.
- Autonomy, competence and social connection are useful questions for observing motivation, not a formula for retaining everyone.
- Give players clear stopping and recovery points. A progression reward is different from improving at the core action.
