# Count the whole system's work

- Total tokens include all workers and synthesis. Lead-context size measures what the lead carries. Outcome efficiency includes time, cost and acceptable quality.
- Parallel independent investigations may shorten elapsed time while increasing aggregate work.
- A tightly coupled debugging chain may work better in one session because each step depends on the previous result.

Toy runs: one worker uses 12,000 tokens in 8 minutes; three workers plus synthesis use 21,000 in 5 minutes. Both meet the same stated criteria.
