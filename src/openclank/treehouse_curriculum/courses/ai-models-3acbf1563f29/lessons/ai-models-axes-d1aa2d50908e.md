# Describe a model on separate axes

- Architecture: how computation is structured, such as a Transformer or convolutional network.
- Training: what data and objective changed its parameters.
- Task: what you want it to do, such as classify or generate.
- Modality: which inputs and outputs it accepts, such as text or images.

An image-generating system can combine several architectures. “Multimodal” alone does not tell you its training method or whether it can generate images.
