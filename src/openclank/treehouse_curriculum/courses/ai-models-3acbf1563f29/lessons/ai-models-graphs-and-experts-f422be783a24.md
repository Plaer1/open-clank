# Graph neighborhoods and internal experts

- A graph neural network can combine a node's features with information from its neighbors. GraphSAGE is one such approach.
- A mixture-of-experts layer routes computation to expert subnetworks; sparse routing uses a subset for a given input.
- Internal experts are model components. Delegated agents are separate model-and-harness work sessions with their own context and tool boundaries.

A molecule's bonds supply graph relationships. Asking two workers to inspect separate documents is external delegation.
